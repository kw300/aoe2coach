## Coach

### Biggest takeaways from this replay

### 1. Upgrades > unit count

Fully-upgraded Cavaliers beat half-upgraded Battle Elephants. If one idea sticks, make it this: a smaller, fully-upgraded army usually beats a bigger, half-upgraded one.

**Habit to steal:** before queuing the next 10 units, ask: "Are the upgrades for these units researching right now?" If not, fix that first.

### 2. Composition must answer the opponent, not the civ's flavor text

Sora played Malay and reached for the headline bonus — cheap Elephants — instead of the correct answer to Khmer cavalry: Arbalest + Halberdier + Mangonel. Civ bonuses are tools, not obligations.

**Habit to steal:** scout the opponent's military buildings in Castle Age. 3 Stables means prepare Pikes/Halbs. 3 Ranges means prepare Skirms. Let the opponent's army dictate the composition.

### 3. Losing the decisive fight ends games

Sora won the first engagement at 22:42. It did not matter. The fight at 32:49 decided everything. Big Castle/Imperial engagements have far more weight than early skirmishes.

**Habit to steal:** do not trade armies in Castle Age unless there is an upgrade edge, numbers edge, or positional edge. Stall with walls, Mangonels, and defensive production until the tech catches up.

### 4. Idle TC matters at every level

Even at 2700+ ELO, Sora had 256s of idle TC, including a 148s gap at 6:31. Hera had even more idle TC and still won because his military decisions were better. Constant villager production is never "solved" forever.

**Habit to steal:** bind Town Centers to a hotkey. Every base check includes a production-queue check.

### 5. EAPM is a real signal

Hera's 131 EAPM vs Sora's 71 was not just "faster clicking." It showed up as continuous reinforcement. Sora reinforced in waves; Hera kept the army stream going.

**Habit to steal:** set production-building hotkeys and cycle through them every 30 seconds, not only when the army is idle.

### 6. Float is wasted economy

Both players floated heavily. The difference: Hera spent into Castles, Stables, and upgrades. Sora's resources sat around while the army stayed under-upgraded.

**Habit to steal:** banking more than 800 of anything in Castle Age means building production, buying a key upgrade, or placing a Castle now.

### 7. Identical openings, different middlegames

Feudal times were 21 seconds apart. Castle times were 15 seconds apart. The game was not decided by the build order. It was decided by what happened between minutes 20 and 33.

Most players obsess over build orders and ignore midgame decision-making. This replay is proof that the opening is the easy part.

**One-sentence version:** above beginner level, games are won by what gets researched and what gets built to answer the opponent — not by who has the fastest Feudal or the biggest army.
